Attack Steps:

Step 1: Exploit Reflected XSS Finding: XSS-001
Attacker sends malicious link to victim with XSS payload in search parameter.
https://example.com/search?q=
Step 2: Steal Session Cookie Finding: COOKIE-001
XSS payload executes and steals session cookie (missing HttpOnly protection).
// Victim's session cookie sent to attacker sessionId=abc123def456; Path=/; Secure
Step 3: Session Hijacking & Account Takeover CRITICAL IMPACT
Attacker uses stolen session cookie to impersonate victim and gain full account access.
⚠
Business Impact:
  • Unauthorized access to user accounts
  • Potential data breach affecting customer PII
  • Financial transactions can be performed as victim
  • Reputation damage and loss of customer trust
  • Regulatory compliance violations (GDPR, PCI-DSS)

🔗 Finding Correlation Links

XSS-001 → COOKIE-001:
Reflected XSS can exploit missing HttpOnly flag to steal session cookies. Combined severity escalated from HIGH to CRITICAL.
SQLi-001 → AUTH-001:
SQL injection enables extraction of password hashes, weak hashing algorithm makes credential compromise trivial. Chain enables admin access.
CORS-001 → XSS-001:
Permissive CORS policy amplifies XSS impact by allowing cross-origin data exfiltration.
CSRF-001 → IDOR-001:
CSRF can be used to trigger IDOR vulnerabilities, enabling unauthorized data modification.

💼 Executive Business Impact Summary

Critical Risks:

  • Complete account takeover capability
  • Full database compromise possible
  • Customer PII exposure risk
  • Payment information at risk
  • Regulatory compliance violations

Business Consequences:

  • GDPR fines: Up to €20M or 4% revenue
  • PCI-DSS non-compliance penalties
  • Data breach notification costs
  • Customer trust & reputation loss
  • Potential legal liability
Recommended Immediate Actions:
  1. Disable affected search functionality until XSS is patched
  2. Implement input validation and output encoding
  3. Add HttpOnly and SameSite flags to all session cookies
  4. Deploy parameterized queries to prevent SQL injection
  5. Implement Content Security Policy (CSP) header
  6. Restrict CORS to specific trusted origins
  7. Conduct emergency security code review
